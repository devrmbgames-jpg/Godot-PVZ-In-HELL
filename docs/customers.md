# Customers, physical delivery and disputes (R11)

`DaySession/C_CustomerFlow` owns persisted `CustomerVisit` and `CustomerComplaint` resources. Records contain stable visit/customer/package IDs and authored service policy, never Node references. In the main scene `customer_id` is a permanent `NpcRecord.npc_id`; one person can have distinct parcel cases across supply days. `C_District` owns the person's identity, placement, schedule and memory. See [Living district](living_district.md). Live `Package -> Customer` binding is exclusively `R_AssignedTo`; displayed registration numbers never establish ownership.

## Schedule and physics

`DEF_CustomerSchedule.events` maps supplied package keys to service policies and arrival delays; `-1` delay disables a case. The authored supply and events determine which cases exist. Registration unlocks the service task, while the person can already exist and walk in the district. Permanent recipient aliases select the living person when the case is first planned; the case keeps that lifetime ID even after death or replacement. Supply day distinguishes cases, not people. Fixtures without `C_District` retain the isolated flow.

`O_CustomerPlanning` prepares the calendar plan from committed day/registration facts and explicit bootstrap commands. `S_CustomerFlow` advances arrival cooldown and fills missing package history IDs; `S_CustomerArrivals` projects `remaining_customer_events` and selects appearances after phase work commits. `NpcServiceRole` attaches one active case to a person; `R_NpcServiceAt` reserves the counter for one served recipient, with other people queued. The shared LimboAI service branch executes the role; departure releases the role and returns the same body to its schedule. Isolated appearance clocks use `S_CustomerClock`; district clocks consume the committed `NpcDecisionReady.delta_seconds` through `O_CustomerServiceClock` before BT decisions. Combat and immediate danger suspend service and release cargo/counter reservations. Unrelated street NPCs do not block shift completion. No completion deletes unrelated packages or invents terminal results. Session Resources are local to each scene instance.

`E_Customer` inherits `E_NpcCharacter` / `E_RigidBodyCharacter` on a Jolt `RigidBody3D`. Player and Customer specialize `physical_character.tscn` and share `CharacterMotionSolver`, `CharacterLookSolver` and impact capture. `C_CustomerAgent` contains visit/phase state only; `CustomerFlowService` issues generic `NpcIntentService` commands. `S_NpcIntent` produces Controller motion/look directions from `C_NpcIntent`; live follow/watch targets use independent `R_NpcMoveTarget` / `R_NpcLookTarget` relationships. Player input markers exclude the Player from the NPC producer. Only the body callback owns physical velocity, with bounded locomotion acceleration/friction preserving external impulses.

Entry and Waiting markers belong to the physical counter. Each NPC has a `NavigationAgent3D`; `S_NpcIntent` follows native waypoints through an optional hazard-aware district route. `NpcIntentArbiter` owns competing movement intentions, while the Jolt body owns velocity and transform. Rebuild `content/domains/npc/authoring/navigation/navmesh_warehouse.tres` after changing district or warehouse static geometry with `godot --headless --path . --script utils/bake_warehouse_navigation.gd`. Ordinary movement never teleports. Visibility limits live watch targets; losing a combat target starts search at its last known position. Existing Idle/Walk clips follow actual body speed. Blocked tasks have bounded timeout and release reservations. Combat uses the existing attack executor and damage observer.

## Player flow

Receiving automatically registers each successfully placed incoming shipment before planning its case. Scanner use is optional and reads its existing number. A package-pickup customer still requires an active registration record; leaving a new shipment unscanned does not block arrival or delivery. Once eligible, the customer approaches, greets and states the registered order number; F repeats the request. Delivery has two equivalent authoritative paths. For a direct handoff, keep a Package in Carry/right/left and aim at the waiting Customer: the higher-priority F action becomes "Передать посылку". For counter delivery, place exactly one released box on the green DeliveryCounter and press F. Both paths reuse the same registration, stable identity, AssignedTo, condition and Customer policy checks. The counter still rejects HELD packages; direct handoff explicitly allows the Player-owned grip. Missing, multiple, unregistered, wrong, unassigned and destroyed packages retain distinct rejection feedback.

Damaged/opened flags remain separate; customer policy may accept with reduced Satisfaction/payment or voluntarily refuse. A valid physical handoff may first begin private/immediate inspection: `READY` confirms handling, not necessarily a delivered actual outcome. Direct handoff releases the Player grip before inspection or the receiver decision. A refused direct parcel is placed at the NPC's authored local `refused_parcel_offset`, with velocities reset once; refused counter parcels stay on the counter. Acceptance releases the warehouse number and removes the physical parcel. Arrival, timeouts and terminal declarations never fetch or teleport storage boxes.

Terminal is package-centric. Each registered Package row shows its warehouse number, status, condition/type hints and accounting value; selection opens detailed package information including the hidden history/debug ID. The outcome actions Taken / Refused / Lost write declaration only: they do not invent the physical/dialogue actual outcome and do not dismiss a live Customer. An initial false Taken is allowed and preserved. Committed declarations cannot be overwritten; repeated commands are idempotent. Player denial is a gameplay/dialogue fact rather than a Terminal button, so lying afterward does not erase the authoritative actual outcome/reputation reason. Search, sort and archive filtering are presentation only; archive means a package outcome has been declared (or a record without a customer visit has physically departed). Package and money history have separate searchable views. Missing/denied parcels retain their registration until an explicit warehouse departure. Unregistered package-pickup visits do not block shift completion because no customer is eligible to arrive yet.

The retained overdue path applies to unnumbered history, including old missing boxes and isolated fixtures, rather than unscanned newly received shipments. Before customer reconciliation, restore registers existing live unnumbered arrival receipts once; missing, closed and declared-LOST boxes are not recreated. On Morning after the authored arrival day, planning marks an unresolved undeclared visit overdue when its retained history record lacks active registration and any still-live parcel is unregistered. It stores `registration_overdue_day` and requests the dedicated missed-registration penalty. This does not create a LOST declaration, close the order, or remove its physical parcel. Existing committed penalties are not undone by recovery. `registration_penalty_committed` and `registration_money_delta` track the separate retryable settlement. Events with `requires_registered_package = false` are excluded.

## Money, refusal and complaints

R10 remains the only monetary writer. Delivered + Taken pays the authored delivery payment scaled by Satisfaction. Honest Lost settles 120%, player refusal 150%, and revealed concealed/fraudulent refusal 200%. Missed registration has a separate `MISSED_REGISTRATION` settlement at the authored policy rate (currently 300%), without changing the package actual/declaration. If the player later declares LOST, the already charged registration amount offsets the ordinary Lost charge down to a minimum of zero. Earlier committed Lost/refusal outcomes cannot be charged a second time by the same dispute, and a late declaration cannot add a second ordinary settlement after a confirmed complaint. Committed operation IDs are durable. Rejected financial operations remain retryable; a matured complaint may remain `PENDING` even with `ignore_delay=true` until Wallet accepts its transaction.

Voluntary refusal is distinct from player denial. Terminal no longer owns Return/Buyout actions. A refused physical parcel remains in the warehouse with its registration reserved until a physical unload/trash/vehicle interaction decides whether it leaves the warehouse or is kept by the player. RETURNED/BOUGHT_OUT remain domain lifecycle values; a Terminal outcome button cannot perform that physical disposal. A legitimate complaint remains independent of it. Per-record player notes use `PackageRegistrationRecord.note`, keyed by `history_id`; `PackageHistoryService.update_note` owns mutation and the closed record contract preserves the text.

Complaint records retain a typed reason (`NOT_DELIVERED` or `DAMAGED`). Delivery stores persistent damaged/opened facts on the visit so delayed damaged-package complaints remain adjudicable after the physical Package is gone. A confirmed damaged complaint currently records a dedicated reputation hook with zero monetary delta; its future monetary penalty is intentionally not hard-coded without a separate game-design value.

Complaint decisions retain the true physical outcome. Successful delivery makes a non-delivery complaint false: the customer-specific record grants a default seven-game-day retaliation reputation window, [resolved_day, resolved_day + 7). A false Taken complaint from a customer defeated by the player before resolution records WAIVED_PLAYER_DEFEAT, keeps the negative fraud reputation reason and charges no money. Other dead claimants have an explicit NO_LIVING_CLAIMANT result. Records remain usable after customer Nodes disappear.

`CustomerVisit.reputation`, positive feedback (`APPROVED`) and complaint windows retain their service meaning. Personal `NpcMemory` separately records understood interactions and witnessed violence; no global reputation or automatic rumor spread exists. Satisfaction does not replace these reasons. Service probabilities and timeouts remain in `DEF_Customer`, while personality and supernatural rules live in `DEF_NpcProfile`. Their incident reactions are cached and never rerolled by reopening dialogue.

## Refusal negotiation and follow-up visits

A waiting package Customer can be told that the Package cannot be issued. DialogueManager response tags are intentionally short authoring metadata: `hon`, `lie`, `prs`, `thr`, `flr`, `jok`. The project-owned dialogue panel displays `[честно]`, `[обман]`, `[убедить]`, `[угроза]`, `[флирт]`, `[шутка]` as presentation prefixes and forwards the original response tags to `CustomerDialogueContext`; `CustomerOutcomeService` applies the matching data-only `DEF_CustomerDialogueReaction`. Tags never encode percentages directly.

Reaction data can change Satisfaction and the Complaint/Aggression/follow-up probability thresholds. Applied intent modifiers are persistent and idempotent per intent category. Joke does not commit a refusal. A committed dialogue refusal records the factual `PLAYER_DENIED` independently from whatever Terminal declaration the Player later chooses.

A finished NPC appearance is not necessarily a resolved package case. For registered cases with declaration `NONE` and actual `NOT_RESOLVED` or `PLAYER_DENIED`, complaint creation is evaluated first. If no complaint is created, a deterministic data-driven follow-up may be scheduled. On its due day the same persistent case is reactivated, current actual returns to `NOT_RESOLVED` so delivery is still possible, while prior denial count and dialogue modifiers remain persistent. Terminal declaration or an existing complaint prevents that pending follow-up.


### Explicit postponement

The persuasion branch "Приходите завтра" is an explicit service postponement, not PLAYER_DENIED. It stores a committed follow-up for the next day and suppresses complaint RNG for that departing appearance. Reactivation still requires an eligible registered case with no declaration, complaint or dead recipient; the promise does not force a physical appearance. Random follow-up logic remains available for unresolved cases that did not receive an explicit promise.

## Fire receiver opening

The typed `DEF_NpcFireAura` supplies a default 0.8 probability of inspecting a received parcel immediately at handoff. `CustomerInspectionService` attaches the real parcel to the NPC's authored `InspectionParcelSlot`, reserves that slot through `R_InspectingAt` and enters the existing `INSPECTING` phase. It requests real `PackageOpening` once; the ordinary authored service tree owns later inspection/return/completion. A failed immediate choice preserves ordinary delivery or the visit's existing private-booth policy. Brute privacy keeps its own authored rule.

`PackageContentPlaced.actor_id` retains the actual opening initiator from the durable pending-loot record. `O_CustomerInspectionCargo` first binds the placed physical item through the still-current reservation, then invokes the explicit fire ignition operation. The operation requires the matching receiver/opener, current order/package, committed opening/release and an eligible physical `C_InventoryItem` definition. The authored list contains fire flask/barrel; unrelated acid, blood and toys cannot trigger it. No closed parcel reveals or materializes its selected item through this policy.

`CustomerVisit.fire_opening_decided` and `fire_opening_explosion` commit the default 0.3 roll before any factory publication. Both outcomes survive codec/save, repeat visits and brain reset; multiple relevant contents and replay cannot reroll or spawn another blast. The request uses a stable package key and receiver attribution, an authored FIRE blast profile and the ordinary Hazard factory/Explosion/damage owners. Fire resistance applies through the existing health owner; outgoing-damage prohibitions preserve their normal Hazard semantics.
